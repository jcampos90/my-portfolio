# Portfolio

The personal site where Juan Campos presents himself to employers and engineering peers, under his own name. It exists alongside the Business site, which sells services to clients, and serves a different audience with different evidence.

## Language

**Portfolio**:
The personal site that presents Juan Campos to employers and engineering peers.
_Avoid_: Personal site, CV site, website

**Business site**:
The client-facing site that sells JcDevSolutions services to prospective clients.
_Avoid_: Main site, marketing site, the website

**Featured project**:
One of the small set of repositories the Portfolio presents with a written account of the work, as distinct from the full inventory of projects on disk.
_Avoid_: Case study, showcase, portfolio item
